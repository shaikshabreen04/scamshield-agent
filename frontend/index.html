Cyber Safety Guidelines

Practical rules to stay protected from phishing, job scams, and fraudulent traps.

💳

Bank KYC & Account Freeze Traps

Scammers claim your account or card will be blocked within 24 hours unless you tap an unverified bit.ly link.

Safety Rule: Real banks never send SMS links to update KYC. Open your official bank app directly.
📦

Fake Delivery & Redelivery Fees

Messages asking you to pay a nominal fee ($1.49 or ₹50) to update your street address for an undelivered parcel.

Safety Rule: The clone website steals your CVV and OTP. Only track shipments on official courier websites.
💼

Telegram Video-Liking & Task Jobs

Promises of ₹3,000–₹5,000 daily for liking videos or rating hotels, requiring an upfront "security deposit".

Safety Rule: Legitimate employers never ask candidates to pay money to get hired or unlock salary withdrawals.
📞

National Cyber Crime Helpline

If you inadvertently entered your banking credentials or transferred funds to a fraudster:

• Call 1930 (India) or visit cybercrime.gov.in

• Freeze your credit/debit card immediately inside your official mobile banking app.
